Install Link Arcade (free, 1 minute)

  1. Click Download extension below, then unzip it (right-click → Extract All).
  2. In Chrome go to chrome://extensions (Edge: edge://extensions).
  3. Turn on Developer mode (top right in Chrome, left side in Edge).
  4. Click Load unpacked and pick the unzipped link-arcade folder.
  5. Click the puzzle-piece icon, pin Link Arcade, and click it anytime to see your bookmarks.

It only reads your bookmarks and visits each bookmarked page once to see if it's still working. Nothing is sent anywhere else, and there's no account or cost.
